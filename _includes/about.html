Our Porous Metal Filtration History.

  • 2005-2010

    Early Stage

    Initially producing simple metal mesh screens and filter plates for local equipment repair shops!

  • 2010-2015

    Defining the Core Business

    After 2010, as industrial filtration needs increased, the factory decided to specialize in metal filter elements. It introduced the first coil mesh machines, spot welders, and basic testing devices!

  • 2015-2020

    Process Improvement & Quality Enhancement

    Starting in 2015, the factory improved welding techniques, pleated filter element production processes, and metal media forming methods!

  • 2020-Present

    Stable Operation & Moderate Upgrading

    After 2020, the factory added semi-automated pleating machines and improved welding fixtures, stabilizing production efficiency!
